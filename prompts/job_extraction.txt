RULES

1. Extract only what the posting explicitly states. Never infer, guess or fill a gap.
   Use null (not "") for anything absent. An empty list for absent lists.

1a. Fields whose description says "exactly one of" are closed sets. Reply with one of the
    listed values character for character — "Part-Time", never "Part time" or "part-time".
    If none of the listed values fit, use null.

1b. job_type is what the ROLE is, not what an ATS field on the page happens to say.
    If the title or body calls it an intern, internship, co-op or summer analyst
    programme, job_type is "Internship" — even where the page also shows
    "Job Type: Part time" or "Employment type: Temporary", which is how Workday and
    Greenhouse label student roles. Those hour/duration labels never override the role.

2. Text fields are copied VERBATIM — title, class_standing_requirement,
   work_authorization, and every skill. No paraphrasing, shortening or tidying.

3. title is the role's name as printed. Do not add a season, a year, or a location
   the page does not put in the title itself.

4. season_year is the term the WORK happens in — "Summer 2027 Internship",
   "runs June-August 2027", "Fall 2026 co-op".
   It is NOT built from a date about the posting. If the only years on the page are
   these, season_year is null:
     - application windows — "posted for two business days from 08/03/2026"
     - posting or requisition dates — "Posted: 08/03/2026", "R231441-1"
     - copyright lines, footers, "last updated" stamps
     - a graduation date inside a requirement

5. class_standing_requirement is the sentence naming a year level, an enrollment
   status, or a degree level — who may apply. A sentence about grades, rigor or
   "strong background" is not one. A sentence about when someone graduates is not one.
   Take the degree LEVEL here and put the FIELD in required_majors:
     "Pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science"
       -> class_standing_requirement: "Pursuing a Bachelor's degree"
       -> required_majors: ["Computer Science"]

6. Skills: extract EVERY item from the Requirements / Qualifications / Skills sections,
   including parenthetical tools — "Experience with scripting (Python, ServiceNow)"
   keeps the parenthetical. Do not skip items that look administrative.
   A skill marked "(preferred)", "nice to have", "bonus", "a plus" or "ideally" goes in
   preferred_skills; everything else goes in required_skills.

7. Salary is numbers only, no symbols or commas. "$25-35/hr" -> salary_min 25,
   salary_max 35, salary_period "hourly".

8. posted_date: convert a relative date using TODAY'S DATE above. "Posted 4 Days Ago"
   with today 2026-08-08 -> "2026-08-04".

9. If the page lists SEVERAL distinct roles, return a JSON array with one object per
   role, each with its own title and skills, sharing company/salary/location/season.
   Do not emit the umbrella page title as its own role. One role -> one object.
